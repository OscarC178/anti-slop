# Music

`assets/music-bed.wav` is built from one HeyGen music-catalogue track, looped once with a
4-second crossfade, trimmed to 52 s, faded out from 48.5 s and loudness-normalised to -16 LUFS.

- Source: HeyGen audio catalogue (`heygen.audio.sounds`), fetched 2026-11-14 with
  `npx hyperframes media-use resolve --type bgm`
- Track ID: `1ad85f3820f64b9e98e7c5938048487e`
- Keep this record: it is the proof of origin if YouTube raises a Content ID claim.

Title:
Anti-Slop: hide AI slop on X, LinkedIn, TikTok and Instagram

Description:
Anti-Slop is a free browser extension that folds AI-labelled and AI-sounding posts out of your X, LinkedIn, TikTok and Instagram feeds. Each hidden post leaves a one-line note saying why, and one click brings it back.

- Runs in your browser. No AI model is called, and no post leaves your device.
- You choose how strict it is, and can add your own words to hide.
- Works in Chrome, Edge, Brave and other Chromium browsers.

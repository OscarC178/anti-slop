Anti-Slop hides AI slop on X (Twitter), LinkedIn, TikTok and Instagram, right inside your feed. It is a free AI content filter and AI post blocker that runs on your device, so your feed shows people again.

WHAT IT HIDES

- Posts the platform itself has already tagged as AI-generated, like the "AI info" label that Instagram shows.
- Posts that read like ChatGPT output, spotted from 22 known signs of AI writing in the text itself.
- Any post that mentions a word or phrase you add to your own list of blocked keywords.

WHY IT HELPS

- Your feed gets shorter and clearer. Expect far fewer engagement-bait threads and recycled LinkedIn motivation posts.
- Mass-produced AI posts are an easy way to spread misinformation, and hiding them cuts down how much of it reaches you.
- AI has real uses. Anti-Slop only goes after low-effort generated filler, and you choose how strict it is.

HOW IT WORKS

- Each post gets a score from the writing signals, and anything over your threshold is folded away behind a small grey bar.
- Click "Show" on that bar whenever you want to read a hidden post, and it stays visible while that page is open.
- Turn any of the 22 signals on or off, and move the slider from 1 (strict) to 10 (only the obvious stuff).
- A toolbar badge counts what was hidden on the page you are on. The scorecard keeps running totals for each site and reason.
- When a borderline post is cut short behind "See more", Anti-Slop opens it so the whole text gets scored.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

- There is no AI model and nothing to sign up for. The checks are plain rules that run inside your browser.
- Post text never leaves your device. Your settings and scorecard are kept in your own browser storage.
- By default it shares anonymous daily counts of hidden posts per site, with nothing that identifies you.
- You can switch that off on first run or at any time with the "Share anonymous counts" switch.

GOOD TO KNOW

- Detection is a best guess from writing patterns, so some human posts that sound like AI may be hidden too.
- Anti-Slop never removes anything from the platform. It only changes what you see on your own screen.
- The code is open source on GitHub, and the privacy policy is on the project website.
